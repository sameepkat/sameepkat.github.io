What are LLMs really?

At first, we need to know about what LLMs really are. They are essentially a decoder-only implementation of Transformer architecture. The Transformer architecture introduced by the Attention is All You Need paper comprised of an encoder-decoder architecture. This encoder-decoder approach was specially useful for things like translation and summarization. Implementation-wise BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representation from Transformers) models are encoder-only, GPT (Generative Pretrained Transformer) are decoder-only and T5 (Text-to-Text Transformer) is both encoder-decoder.

The focus of this article is going to be GPT, the Decoder only architecture. Given some text, the decoder part is responsible for predicting the next likely token among the vocabulary. It does this by sampling from top-k most likely words over the vocabulary. This is known as autoregressive generation.
